Shaco's Lair

Privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026.

What this site and app are

Shaco's Lair is a YouTube channel that publishes League of Legends clip compilations. The "Shacos Lair" Google application is a private tool that uploads those episodes to the channel's own YouTube account. It is used only by the channel owner. Nobody else signs in to it.

What data the app uses

The app asks for permission to manage the channel's YouTube videos (the youtube.upload and youtube scopes). It uses that permission only to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, privacy and schedule, and read back the status of videos it uploaded. It does not read comments, subscriber lists, analytics or anything from other channels.

Where the data goes

The app runs on the channel owner's own computer. The sign-in token is stored there and nowhere else. No data is sent to any third party, sold, or used for advertising.

Website visitors

This website has no accounts, no cookies of its own, no analytics and no forms. It is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs, described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Clips and streamers

Episodes use publicly available Twitch clips. Every streamer is credited and linked in the video description. If you are a streamer and want a clip removed, email shacoslair@gmail.com and it will be taken out.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account permissions.
