Shaco's Lair

Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026.

What this site and app are

Shaco's Lair is a YouTube channel that publishes League of Legends clip compilations. The "Shacos Lair" Google application is a private tool that uploads those episodes to the channel's own YouTube account. It is used only by the channel owner. Nobody else signs in to it.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of the data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app uses

The app asks for permission to manage the channel's YouTube videos (the youtube.upload and youtube scopes). It uses that permission only to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, privacy and schedule, and read back the status of videos it uploaded. It does not read comments, subscriber lists, analytics or anything from other channels.

Where the data goes

The app runs on the channel owner's own computer. What it stores there: the sign-in token, and for each video it uploaded, the video ID together with the title, privacy setting and schedule the app itself set, so nothing is uploaded twice. Video status read back from YouTube is shown on screen and not stored. Nothing is stored anywhere else. No data is sent to any third party, sold, or used for advertising.

Keeping and deleting data

Deleting the app's data folder on the owner's computer deletes everything it stored, and deleting the token ends its access straight away. In addition to that, you can revoke the app's access to your data at any time on the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. To ask what is stored or to have it deleted, email shacoslair@gmail.com.

Website visitors

This website has no accounts, no cookies of its own, no analytics and no forms. It is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs, described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Clips and streamers

Episodes use publicly available Twitch clips. Every streamer is credited and linked in the video description. If you are a streamer and want a clip removed, email shacoslair@gmail.com and it will be taken out.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service, the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
